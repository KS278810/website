Research Overview

Pioneering the intersection of mechanical design and machine learning to achieve breakthrough performance and design flexibility.

Generative AI for 3D Shape Design

Bridging aesthetic ideation and rigorous engineering constraints through deep learning. By leveraging implicit representations like Signed Distance Functions (SDF), my work enables AI models to directly predict aerodynamics and physical performance metrics, empowering seamless generation of both optimal and beautiful 3D topologies (e.g., VehicleSDF, WheelSDF).

Set-Based Concurrent Engineering

Moving beyond point-based trial and error by mathematically defining feasible design regions early in the development cycle. I actively research and implement Bayesian Active Learning frameworks, integrating Gaussian process surrogate models to drastically reduce computational loads and discover robust operational parameters for complex multi-physics systems.

Structural Optimization

Exploring the theoretical boundaries of non-parametric shape optimization and topology optimization. My past and ongoing research focuses on critical manufacturing reliability, from solving minimax cost functions for brake squeal suppression to robust multi-material optimization for space structures like ultra-large millimeter-wave telescope components under extreme gravity variations.
