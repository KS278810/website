Surrobot

A browser-based AutoML tool I built for training and deploying regression models entirely client-side, powered by Pyodide (Python compiled to WebAssembly) — no server, no data ever leaves your machine. Drop in a CSV, and it automatically compares linear, gradient-boosted tree, Gaussian process, and neural network models to find the best fit, then lets you export a standalone predictor.
