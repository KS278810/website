Bridging Automotive Engineering & Machine Learning

I am an AI Researcher and PhD in Information Science, bridging physical intelligence and data-driven innovation. My work integrates PDE-based deductive approaches with inductive AI methodologies to create high-performance engineering designs.

With a background as a Manager at Toyota and Visiting Researcher at the University of Tokyo, I currently drive 3D generative design and AI-driven simulation as a Technical Advisor at Toyota Research Institute (TRI).

I am a strong advocate for Set-Based Concurrent Engineering, developing Bayesian Active Learning frameworks that eliminate development re-work by mathematically identifying feasible design regions early in the cycle.
